# Demonstration video

Title: Tidal Loom — a water garden you mend by hand (WebXR / IWER demo)

Turn the channels, follow the spring and help every garden bloom. Tidal Loom is a small seated puzzle with three islands, branching water, growing flowers and optional synthesized chimes.

This 1:18.90 demo combines actual desktop play and Meta IWER hand-simulator footage. The simulator close-ups are cropped and labeled. It shows a pinch-and-twist completing a channel, hand-ray access to the board's Undo and Next controls, and the second and third puzzles. There is no audio track or generated gameplay. Physical headset comfort, hand tracking and performance remain unverified.
