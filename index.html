A spring for every garden

Each round island has channels that can be turned a quarter turn. Water starts at the small spring, then follows channels that meet at an edge.

Connect every garden and close any leaking path to finish the island. Dry rock cannot be turned. Undo and Start again are always available.

In VR, stay seated: the board is within a small reach. Pinch and twist a channel, then release. A short pinch makes one quarter turn. The board’s labeled controls work with a hand ray.

Progress stays on this device. No account, cloud model, purchase, or personal data is needed to play. Simulator controls appear only when you explicitly open the hand simulator.
